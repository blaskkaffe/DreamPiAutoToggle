Brightness
White balance: tap Preview on LED to show solid white, then turn down whichever of R, G or B looks too strong until it looks neutral - leave the others at 255. Tap Preview again when done. This corrects every colour the LEDs show, not just white, and takes about 30 seconds. Brightness is the maximum for the whole strip, applied after the correction (logarithmic: the left half is the low range that suits an indicator LED).

MessageColourEffectLevel
Every message is set up on its own: tick it to use it, pick its colour, effect and level, and (with a strip) which LEDs it uses. Many start out alike on purpose; they are separate so you can change one or send it to its own LED. Orange is DCNow!, blue is DCNET, purple is Netlink, and red means something is wrong. Errors always win over information, and State unknown only shows when nothing else applies. Messages marked not detected yet can be set up, but DreamPi doesn't tell this add-on when they happen, so they don't show. Effect: Solid, Blink, Breathe, Ping (two quick pulses, then a pause), RGB, or with a strip an animation, plus its speed. Level: its own brightness; grey means the maximum brightness above. The status dot on the main page previews the DreamPi message that is showing.
