zones.json - the time zone areas of the clock's world map

Made by tools/build_clock_zones.py from timezone-boundary-builder by Evan Siroky
(https://github.com/evansiroky/timezone-boundary-builder), release 2026b, the "timezones-now" set
(areas that keep the same clock time from now on). The areas are simplified to about a third of a degree, cut to
latitudes 60 S to 78 N, and small islands are left out.
