# The check-in board and the contacts

Read before touching `modules/checkin/`, `modules/contacts/`, the `roster` widget (`base/page/widgets.js`) or the kiosk script. Back to [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md). The idea and most of the features come from CheckinChicken (a Node.js check-in board); this is the same board as two modules of the add-on's module base.

## How it fits together

- **One host, any number of screens.** The computer that ran `install.sh` is the host: its web service holds the roster and who is in. Every other screen is only a browser that opens the host's address (full screen, in kiosk mode if you like: `kiosk/kiosk-browser.sh <host>`), so they show the same thing. A screen can also be the host itself.
- **In step in real time.** `api()` of the check-in module puts the whole board (`d["checkin"]`: groups, people, the status menu, buildings, a revision) into every `GET /api` answer, which every page asks for once a second, and a tap POSTs and redraws at once from the answer. A change on one screen is on all of them within about a second. The revision (`"<state revision>.<roster stamp>"`) changes with every tap, status, setting and import, so the widget redraws only when something changed. No socket, no server-sent events: nothing to keep alive over a flaky Wi-Fi link. If the polling ever becomes a limit, a long-poll on `/checkin?since=<rev>` is the next step (the revision is there for it).
- **No standalone server needed**, but possible: the web service is plain Python 3 and runs anywhere (`python3 base/base_web.py 8080 0` after pointing the paths in `base/base_core.py` somewhere writable; or `python3 tests/ui/demo_server.py` for a sandbox with sample people). Syncing the state between **several hosts** (every screen its own server) is not built: all state is in `checkin.json` / `contacts.json` and every change goes through one lock and one atomic write, which is the shape a later peer-to-peer sync would need.

## Contacts (`modules/contacts/`)

- **Import** (Settings > Contacts): a CSV file (chosen with the app's own "Choose file" button; UTF-8, or Windows-1252 when it is not valid UTF-8) or pasted text, `POST /contacts/import {"csv", "replace"}` (PIN-protected when a PIN is set). Columns as in CheckinChicken's `people.csv`: `name, department, role, phone, location, restrictToLocation`. `location` is the **building** (CheckinChicken's "Område" / coop). Lines that start with `#` and blank lines are skipped; comma, semicolon and tab are found from the header line; a BOM is ignored; a chosen file is read as UTF-8, or as Windows-1252 when it is not valid UTF-8 (a plain "CSV" save from Excel on a Swedish PC), so å, ä and ö survive either way (the decoding is in the browser, `modules/contacts/page.js`; checked with Node's TextDecoder, not by picking a file in a browser); Swedish headers (`namn`, `avdelning`, `roll`, `telefon`, `omrade`) and `building` are accepted. `restrictToLocation` is `1/true/yes/ja/x`.
- **Editing a person:** a tap on a row of the people list (not on its switch) opens a pop-up with name, department, role, phone, building and "only show when this building is chosen" (`POST /contacts/person`, PIN-protected; the id stays, so the in / out state, status and photo stay; a CSV import finds the person by the values they have now, so an edited person is not added twice; **Delete person** at the bottom of the editor (first tap asks "Tap again to delete", the second removes the person and their photo: `POST /contacts/delete`, PIN-protected; the status of a deleted person stays in `checkin.json` unused; a CSV import that has the same name, department and building brings them back as a new person); a name that is empty or a double of another person in the same department and building is refused with a message). **Statuses that need a time, date or note** ask for it in a second pop-up over the menu with a big field (Enter sets it, Esc or a tap outside goes back). The check-in options Title / Group people by / Colour people by are `inline` fields: the switch or the drop-down is on the row.
- **Role and building on the rows:** the small grey text at the right of a row is the role and / or the building ("Chef · Område A"). Settings > Check-in board has **Display roles** (on by default) and **Display buildings** (off), each a switch with an **Edit** button that lists the roles / buildings on file as ticked boxes (all ticked = all shown; `config.roles_shown` / `buildings_shown` are `null` or the list of the ticked ones, `show_roles` / `show_buildings` the switches; the board snapshot carries all four). **Box titles** are 1em (the size of the lists in Settings) and bold. **The carousel** of a row with a status repeats its text enough times to fill the row (the track moves by half its length), so it also works in stretched, wide boxes; it is measured again when the screen layout or window changes.
- **Look** (Settings > Check-in board): **Page title** (`config.title`, empty = the project's; sets the `h1` and the browser tab), **Box frame** (none / thin / thick: `data-frame` on `.rp-box`) and **Box colour** (`config.box`: **Grey, like the theme** (`auto`, the default: the original dark grey in the dark theme, light grey in the light theme), **Dark grey (the original)**, **Light grey**, or **The board colour**: the frame and the title row of every box take the colour of Appearance > Check-in board colour, `.rp-tile.rp-col`). The **building buttons** are in the top row at the left of the title, as tall as the cogwheel; when they do not fit there (a narrow screen) they are one button ("All" or the number chosen) that opens the list. **Appearance > Hide the top bar** (`screen.json` `autohide`, `POST /screen/autohide`): the top row (buttons, title, cogwheel) is out of sight and the first box starts at the top of the screen; it comes down when the pointer is at the top edge or the edge is tapped, stays while the pointer is on it or the building list is open, and goes up again after about four seconds. The **text colour** (Appearance > Text colour, `screen.json` `font_colour`: `auto` (the default), black, white or a palette colour) is `--rp-text`. The boxes have two tones, set by the page on `<html>` (`fontLook()` in `page.js`): `data-rows=dark` is the original look (dark grey card `#1b1b1b`, dark grey rows while out with grey text, the strong colour with white text while in) and `data-rows=light` a light grey card with light shades of the colours and black text. The tone comes from **Box colour**; with the automatic text colour the text is the tone's own (`data-font=auto`), with a colour picked by hand it is that colour (`data-font=custom`) and, unless the box colour is fixed, a dark colour gives light boxes. A status that asks for a time or a date, without the touch keyboard, is typed as 24-hour `tt:mm` and `åååå-mm-dd` (text fields with a mask, not the browser's own fields, which follow the browser's language).
- **Scrolling text** (Settings > Check-in board > Scrolling text, `config.scroll`: `off`, `auto`, `status` ("Auto, only the status"), `on`, default `on`): with a status, `on` always scrolls the name and status round like a carousel, `off` shows the name with the status as plain text at the right, `status` keeps the name where it is and gives the status its own small area at the right (`.rp-sm`), which scrolls only when the status is too long for it, and `auto` keeps a status that fits the row still (one copy, `.rp-mq.still`) and scrolls only one that does not (measured again when the row changes size).
- **Touch keyboard** (Settings > Check-in board > Touch keyboard, `config.keyboard`, off by default): the pop-up for a status that needs a time, a date or a note shows an on-screen keyboard instead of the browser's field: a number pad for a time (typed as HHMM, stored as `HH:MM`, 24 h) and for a date (DDMM, the year is this year or next year if the date has passed), and a text keyboard laid out like the iPhone's Swedish one (q w e r t y u i o p å / a s d f g h j k l ö ä / shift z x c v b n m delete / 123, emoji, mellanslag, klar; the 123 layer has numbers and punctuation with `kr`, `#+=` has more symbols; the emoji layer has eight groups; large keys, a wide pop-up) for a note. With the setting off the browser's own time / date / text field is used (a native time field follows the browser's language, not the Clock module's setting). The status text on a row still shows times as 24 h. Checked in a browser (`tests/ui/functional.js`); not on a touch screen.
- **Statuses editor** (Settings > Statuses, custom widget in `modules/checkin/page.js`; the list is `checkin.json` `statuses`, `POST /checkin/statuses {"statuses": [...]}` replaces it, at most 16, PIN-protected): tap a status to change its name, colour (a palette colour), what it asks for (nothing, a time, a date, a note), the text before a date ("back"), the start time of the time field, **dots** next to the name on the board (1-3, CheckinChicken's "plupp"), **counts as out** (`out`: picking it checks the person out) and **sticky**; the arrows reorder, Add status adds one (it gets a fresh code), Delete asks first. **Sticky:** when INNE / UTE is pressed a sticky status stays and any other status is cleared (the default for every status is not sticky, as in CheckinChicken); a person whose status was deleted shows none. The fixed Inne / Ute pair and the Dag / Vecka choice for dates are not editable / not ported. The editors use a font of 1.25em in 50 px fields; **Delete** in the person and status editors asks in a pop-up (`askConfirm()` in `base/page/page.js`) and sits to the left of Cancel.
- **People list:** Settings > Contacts lists everyone on file with a switch for "on the board" (`POST /contacts/active {id, value}`; a switched-off person leaves the board, the data stays).
- **Merging, never deleting:** a person is found again by building + department + name (`person_id()`, a short hash), updated in place and kept in step with their check-in state; people not in the file stay unless "Switch off people that are not in the file" is ticked (they get `active: false` and leave the board; the data stays). A person with no department gets "No department". At most 2000 people.
- **Files:** `contacts.json` (`contacts_web.CONTACTS`: `{"people": [{id, name, department, role, phone, location, restrictToLocation, active, order}]}`), written only by this module. `GET /contacts` (the count text for Settings), `GET /contacts.csv` (export), `python3 modules/contacts/contacts_web.py people.csv [--replace]` imports from a shell.
- The board reads `contacts.json` directly, so it works while the contacts module is off (only the import box and the endpoints are gone).

## Check-in board (`modules/checkin/`)

- **State** `checkin.json` (`checkin_web.CHECKIN`): `{"rev", "config": {group_by, colour_by, colours}, "statuses": null | [...], "people": {id: {in, status, detail, at}}}`. Everything goes through `_lock` and an atomic write (`_save()` bumps `rev`).
- **A person's row:** grey while out; the **INNE** (green) / **UTE** (red) button at its right switches in <-> out (the state is on the button) and a tap anywhere else on the row opens the status menu; in = the whole row in the colour of the person's department or building (`colour_by`); a status gives the row the **status' own colour**, and while a status is set the name and the status scroll round like a carousel (`"Erik Lindqvist · Kommer sent · 08:15"`). A status that is `out` (Sjuk ...) also shows UTE. `POST /checkin/toggle {id}` (in <-> out, **keeps the status**), `POST /checkin/status {id, code, detail}` (`code` a status code, `""` = clear; `IN` / `OUT` still work), `POST /checkin/all {in}` (everybody in / out, statuses cleared).
- **Statuses** are CheckinChicken's menu, Swedish labels, with palette colours: FYS, Kommer sent (time), Går tidigare (time), Tjänsteresa (date, "tillbaka"), Sjuk, Jobbar hemifrån, Semester (date), VAB, Läkarbesök (time), Föräldraledig, Personlig dag, Annat (a note). A status that is `out` (Sjuk, Semester ...) also checks the person out; the others keep in / out. INNE / UTE changes in / out without touching the status. The list is `DEFAULT_STATUSES` in `checkin_web.py`, replaced by `checkin.json` `"statuses"` (a list of `{code, label, colour, needs: time|date|note, default, prefix, out}`, at most 16, colours palette ids). A detail is cleaned on the server (a time is `HH:MM`, a date becomes `d/m`, a note at most 60 characters). Not ported from CheckinChicken: the "plupp" dot statuses and the admin editor for the menu.
- **Colours are palette ids only** (`AUTO` cycles through twelve of them by the sorted department / building name; Settings > Check-in board lists every department and building with a select to give it one, `POST /checkin/colour {kind, name, colour}`; "Automatic" goes back). `colour_by` and `group_by` are `department` or `building`, so "department and/or building" is a setting.
- **Tiles and columns:** every department (or building) box is a tile of the main screen, made and removed by the `roster` widget as the groups change (`data-box="checkin-<name>"`), so Appearance > Max columns / Stretch / Scale and Rearrange apply to them like to any tile; the board's own tile holds the buildings chips. With Max columns 2 or 3 the departments fill the columns in turn. Dragging a department tile saves the order of the departments on the host (`config.group_order` in `checkin.json`, `POST /checkin/order` with the names, a protected path; the board snapshot is sorted by it, names not in it follow alphabetically), so every screen shows the same order. **Long groups:** with more than one column, the widget measures a row and the screen height left under the dashboard and cuts a group with more people than fit in a column into near-equal parts, one tile each (`id-p2`, ... ; titles "Kök (1/2)"), which the columns take in turn; it measures again on a window resize. In one column nothing is cut. Appearance > **No scrolling** (`screen.json` `noscroll`, `POST /screen/noscroll`) makes `html.noscroll` hide the scroll of the main screen (Settings still scrolls). Checked in a browser with `tests/ui/columns.js` (30 people in one department at 1920x1080, 3 columns); not on a kiosk screen.
- **The look** (the `roster` widget): one box per group (department or building, `group_by`) in a single column; the box's top row names the group and counts who is in (`3/10`); under it a row per person, large text (1.75em), rows full width to the box edge with thin dividers, the name on the left, the status or the role on the right and the small **Status** button inside the row. Sized so that 60 people fit a 1080p screen once the page has columns (checked with temporary CSS before the columns came from `development`: three columns in landscape, two in portrait, about 1010 px of 1080 and 1920 used); the page has as many columns as Appearance > Max columns allows.
- **Status menu:** a pop-up in the middle of the screen, about 60 % of its width (the whole width on a phone), with a dimmed backdrop. At the top the person: **photo** (initials in a coloured circle until there is one; the picture is chosen in Settings > Contacts, not here), name, department and building, role, phone; a cross in the top right corner (a click outside or Esc closes it too). Below, every status as a button of one size, three in a row, in its colour, and **Rensa status** (clear the status) centred under them; a status that needs a time / date / note first shows the field with the default (`07:30` ...) and a **Set** button.
- **Buildings filter:** with two or more buildings a chip row ("All", one per building) picks what this screen shows; the choice is kept in this browser (`localStorage` `checkin:locations`) and `?location=A,B` in the address sets it (`kiosk-browser.sh <host> "Område A"`). People with `restrictToLocation` show only when their own building is picked, never under "All"; people with no building show everywhere they are not restricted.
- **Title:** Settings > Check-in board > Title switches the page's "Check-in" heading off (`config.show_title`, in the board data, `body.no-title`): the board then starts at the top and only the cogwheel stays.
- **Settings** also has the colour of the module (Appearance), "All out" / "All in" (tap twice).
- **Photos:** `POST /contacts/photo {id, photo: "data:image/jpeg|png;base64,..."}` (empty = remove; at most 150 KB, checked by its first bytes, stored as `/opt/checkin-board/photos/<id>.jpg|png`, written by the contacts module), `GET /contacts/photo/<id>` serves it (cached an hour; the board's `photo` url carries a version so a new picture shows at once). The board has the urls, never the pictures, so `/api` stays small. Not PIN-protected (anyone who can open the page can set a photo).
- **Not ported yet** from CheckinChicken: the admin passcode screen (the optional PIN of this add-on guards the import), per-screen titles, the themes, the on-screen keyboard and the admin editor for statuses and people.

## Dates, times, notes: the pop-up, the pads and the keyboard (second look)
- A status can ask for **a time**, **a date**, **a date and a time** (`needs: "datetime"`, typed as `åååå-mm-dd tt:mm` or DDMMHHMM on the pad; stored in the status text as `24/12 08:30`), or **a note**. A date and a date-and-time pop-up have a **Skip** button next to Cancel and Set: the status is set without the date (`POST /checkin/status` with an empty detail; the server keeps `""`).
- **Calendar:** a date has a calendar button (📅) to the right of the number field (the typed field, or the number pad's display); it opens a month (Monday first, Swedish names), arrows for the month, a tap on a day fills the field (the pad keeps the year of a day picked in another year).
- **Number pad:** three even columns, every key the same size, a blank key where there is no AM / PM; with several kinds (time, date, date and time) the display shows `tt : mm`, `dd / mm` or both.
- **Photos** are chosen only in **Settings > Contacts** (the person's editor: Choose photo / Remove photo; `POST /contacts/photo`), never from the board.
- **A USB keyboard works with the on-screen one:** digits, letters (å ä ö too), Backspace and Enter (done / Set) are taken from the physical keyboard while a pad or keyboard is open (a / p switch AM / PM on a 12-hour pad); the two can be mixed.
- Checked in Chromium (`tests/ui/functional.js`); not on a touch screen, and not with a real USB keyboard (key events are sent by the test).

## Tall screens: two columns, 20 to 80 people
Tested at 1080 x 1920 with Stretch, two columns and Fit to screen (`tests/ui/portrait.js`, rosters from `tests/ui/make_people.py`: 20, 40, 60 and 80 people in groups of 1 to 15, mostly 6 to 12; the 40, 60 and 80 files have a group of 15 and a group of 1). Everybody is on the screen at once, in two columns of about equal height, every name whole.
- **The columns are shared out by height** (no saved places): each box, the tallest first, goes to the column that is shortest so far, and each column keeps the boxes in their own order (`layoutDash()` in `page.js`). Boxes the user has moved keep their saved places.
- **Fit to screen also makes the text smaller when the page is too tall** at the normal size (never bigger; down to 0.45 times, stopping at the Text size chosen): 80 people on a 1080 x 1920 screen come out at about 0.85 times; with fewer people the rows grow instead (20 people: tall rows, text unchanged). Without Fit, choose a Text size that fits.
- A role gives way before a name does: in a narrow column the role is cut (with an ellipsis), the name is whole.
